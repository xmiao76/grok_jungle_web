AI strength

You always play Red, on the bottom. Off: Blue opens from the top. Flip later is view-only.

How to play

Goal: win by moving any piece into the opponent’s den, or by capturing every enemy animal.

Moving: Red sits on the bottom. Click one of your pieces, then a highlighted square — jade dot = move, cinnabar ring = capture. Pieces step one square orthogonally.

Ranks from rat (1) to elephant (8): rat, cat, dog, wolf, leopard, tiger, lion, elephant. Equal or lower rank captures. An untrapped rat takes an elephant. An elephant cannot take an untrapped rat.

  • Rivers: only the rat swims. A swimming rat captures another rat in the water. A capture cannot cross the bank in either direction.
  • Jumps: a lion on land may jump two or three water squares. A tiger jumps exactly two. A rat of either color on the water blocks the jump.
  • Traps: a piece on an opponent trap has rank 0. Any attacker can capture it. A trapped attacker cannot capture an untrapped defender. Your own traps do not weaken you.
  • Dens: you cannot enter your own den. Entering the enemy den wins.

The game also ends when the opponent has no pieces, when the opponent has no legal move, when the same position (the board and the side to move) occurs for the third time, or after 100 moves with no capture. The first three are wins. The last two are draws.

Controls: Undo takes back your unanswered move, or your move and the AI reply. In AI vs AI it takes back one ply. Flip (or F) changes only the view. Mute, Resign, Menu. Hard thinks for at most one second.
